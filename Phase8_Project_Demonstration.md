# Phase 8: Project Demonstration Phase

## Suggested Demonstration Flow (3–5 minutes)
- **Introduction** – "LegalEase is an AI legal document generator that drafts, previews, edits and exports, powered by Gemini."
- **Start-up** – launch the FastAPI backend with Uvicorn, then the Streamlit frontend.
- **Home page** – show the logo, title and the four input fields.
- **Enter details** – Freelance Work Contract; Jane Doe (Service Provider), TechNova Inc. (Client); sample terms; April 15, 2025.
- **Generate** – click Generate Document and scroll through the preview.
- **Edit** – click Click to Edit Document and change a line of text.
- **Download** – export .TXT, .DOCX and .PDF and show the logo, footer and terms table.
- **Conclusion** – recap the flow: input -> FastAPI -> Gemini -> formatted document.
